**Tools:** STABL, DPLR2D, PSE-Chem, MATLAB, Tecplot

## The problem

Predicting where a hypersonic boundary layer transitions from laminar to turbulent is critical to vehicle design. At the same location on the vehicle, heat transfer to the skin is roughly **three to five times higher** when the flow is turbulent than when it is laminar. Transition location therefore drives the design of the thermal protection system.

Real vehicle surfaces are not held at a single uniform temperature. This project asks how **non-uniform wall temperature profiles** change the boundary layer and the predicted transition location.

## What I did

- Ran mean-flow simulations of a sharp cone in hypersonic flight with **DPLR2D**, part of the Stability and Transition Analysis for Hypersonic Boundary Layers (**STABL**) suite.
- Ran linear stability analysis with **PSE-Chem** to track the growth of boundary-layer instabilities under different wall temperature distributions.
- Post-processed and compared results with **MATLAB** scripts and **Tecplot** visualizations.
- Presented the results at the [2026 Purdue Summer Undergraduate Research Symposium](/talks/2026-07-30-purdue-surf).
