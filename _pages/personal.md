## Where I'm from

I was born in Waco, Texas in 2006 and mostly grew up in South Bend, IN. I have combined these experiences with my dad's ideas to make me a fan of the Dallas Cowboys and Notre Dame. I have an older brother, James, who recently graduated from IU and is preparing to attend medical school. I also have a dog, Mila, who is a Shar-Pei.

## What I do when I'm not doing engineering

When I am not doing engineering, I enjoy being active. I spend most mornings in the gym, whether by myself or with friends, to start the day. I played soccer growing up, captaining my high school team, and I enjoy both playing and watching sports.

I am also a big fan of the culinary industry. Growing up, my brother and I taught ourselves to cook, and have since expanded our skills as we have grown up. In high school, I worked for a local Mexican grill as a chef, which I thoroughly enjoyed. Together, my brother and I have made everything, from smoked brisket to homemade nachos with fresh queso and guacamole.
